## 🚀 Featured Analytics Projects

### 📉 [Revenue Leakage Detection & Root-Cause Analysis](https://revenue-leakage-detector-fdhjfe57xck4qnp4mpx9ge.streamlit.app/)
Analyzed 98,665 e-commerce transactions using SQL and Python to detect anomalous payment patterns, uncovering $2,867 in revenue leakage that had gone undetected. Re-engineered the detection approach with a new payment-residual metric, improving leakage-case coverage from 2% to 100% (1 of 50 → 50 of 50 cases caught). Root-cause analysis found orders paid in 11 installments leaked revenue at 672x the baseline rate due to floating-point rounding errors — a clear, actionable fix for finance and engineering teams.
**Tech Stack:** SQL, Python, Pandas, Streamlit
🔗 [Live Demo](https://revenue-leakage-detector-fdhjfe57xck4qnp4mpx9ge.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/Revenue-Leakage-Detector)

### 📞 [Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Dashboard](https://telecom-churn-predictor-frerjdjk4xqsdjqppjeh27.streamlit.app/)
Analyzed 7,043 customer records across 38 features to identify the strongest churn drivers (89.1% AUC, 81% accuracy) and built a churn-flagging process that correctly identified 294 of 374 at-risk customers (78.6% recall) for the retention team to target. Stress-tested results against a holdout set and caught a case where cross-validation metrics overstated real-world performance, correcting it before results were shared. Designed a 4-page Power BI dashboard and an interactive Streamlit app translating churn drivers into plain-language retention recommendations.
**Tech Stack:** Python, LightGBM, Power BI, Streamlit
🔗 [Live Demo](https://telecom-churn-predictor-frerjdjk4xqsdjqppjeh27.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/telecom-churn-predictor)

### 📰 [News Content Classification & Trend Analysis Dashboard](https://fake-news-predictor-xkb99qxnjhwwyzczmclstd.streamlit.app/)
Cleaned and structured 44,910 news articles into a balanced (~52/48%) dataset with an 80/10/10 train/validation/test split for reliable reporting. Built an interactive dashboard that classifies content as credible or misleading in real time, giving content-review teams a fast, data-backed way to flag misleading articles. Compared multiple approaches to identify the most effective method (up to 99.87% accuracy, outperforming baselines by up to 4 points), then selected a lightweight, interpretable model (0.999 ROC-AUC) balancing accuracy with faster, more cost-effective reporting turnaround.
**Tech Stack:** Python, TF-IDF, DistilBERT, NLTK, Streamlit
🔗 [Live Demo](https://fake-news-predictor-xkb99qxnjhwwyzczmclstd.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/Fake-News-Predictor)

### 🎯 [Sales Lead Conversion & Prioritization Analysis](https://lead-conversion-scorer-n6qhzrfat2cgvigopy5km3.streamlit.app/)
Cleaned and analyzed a 9,240-lead dataset (narrowed from 37 to 23 meaningful features) to identify the factors most strongly associated with conversion (97.9% AUC, 93.6% accuracy). Delivered a lead-scoring analysis with 92.3% recall and 91.3% precision, enabling the sales team to prioritize high-potential leads well above the 38.5% baseline conversion rate.
**Tech Stack:** Python, XGBoost, Streamlit
🔗 [Live Demo](https://lead-conversion-scorer-n6qhzrfat2cgvigopy5km3.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/Lead-Conversion-Scorer)
