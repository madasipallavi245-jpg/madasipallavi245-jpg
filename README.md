## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Business & Data Analytics Analyst** who turns messy, real-world data into insights business teams can act on. Coming from a background in digital marketing before moving into analytics, I bring a business-first lens to my work — I care as much about *why* a number matters to the business as *how* it was calculated. I'm currently focused on building and polishing a portfolio of **end-to-end analytics projects**, from revenue leakage detection and churn prediction to lead scoring, and deploying each one as a live, interactive Streamlit app.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📉 [Revenue Leakage Detection & Root-Cause Analysis](https://revenue-leakage-detector-fdhjfe57xck4qnp4mpx9ge.streamlit.app/)
Analyzed 98,665 e-commerce transactions using SQL and Python to detect anomalous payment patterns, uncovering $2,867 in revenue leakage and improving leakage-case detection coverage from 2% to 100%.
**Tech Stack:** Python, SQL, Scikit-learn, Streamlit
🔗 **Live Demo:** [Launch App](https://revenue-leakage-detector-fdhjfe57xck4qnp4mpx9ge.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 **GitHub:** [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/Revenue-Leakage-Detector)

### 📞 [Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Dashboard](https://telecom-churn-predictor-frerjdjk4xqsdjqppjeh27.streamlit.app/)
Analyzed 7,043 customer records to identify churn drivers (89.1% AUC, 78.6% recall), paired with a 4-page Power BI dashboard translating findings into retention recommendations for business stakeholders.
**Tech Stack:** Python, LightGBM, Power BI, Streamlit
🔗 **Live Demo:** [Launch App](https://telecom-churn-predictor-frerjdjk4xqsdjqppjeh27.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 **GitHub:** [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/telecom-churn-predictor)

### 📰 [News Content Classification & Trend Analysis](https://fake-news-predictor-xkb99qxnjhwwyzczmclstd.streamlit.app/)
Cleaned and analyzed 44,910 news articles to classify content as credible or misleading, reaching 99.87% accuracy and 0.999 ROC-AUC while selecting a lightweight, interpretable model for scalable reporting.
**Tech Stack:** Python, TF-IDF, DistilBERT, NLTK, Streamlit
🔗 **Live Demo:** [Launch App](https://fake-news-predictor-xkb99qxnjhwwyzczmclstd.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 **GitHub:** [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/Fake-News-Predictor)

### 🎯 [Sales Lead Conversion & Prioritization Analysis](https://lead-conversion-scorer-n6qhzrfat2cgvigopy5km3.streamlit.app/)
Analyzed a 9,240-lead dataset to identify the factors most strongly associated with conversion (97.9% AUC, 93.6% accuracy), enabling sales teams to prioritize high-potential leads above the 38.5% baseline rate.
**Tech Stack:** Python, XGBoost, Streamlit
🔗 **Live Demo:** [Launch App](https://lead-conversion-scorer-n6qhzrfat2cgvigopy5km3.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 **GitHub:** [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/Lead-Conversion-Scorer)

### 🤖 [AI Career Mentor — RAG Chatbot "Alex"](https://ai-career-mentor-9xhvufgb2aksdg3jlknptm.streamlit.app/)
A Retrieval-Augmented Generation chatbot that acts as a personalized career mentor, using FAISS vector search and MiniLM embeddings for retrieval, powered by Groq's LLaMA 3.3 70B for generation.
**Tech Stack:** Python, FAISS, MiniLM Embeddings, Groq (LLaMA 3.3 70B), Streamlit
🔗 **Live Demo:** [Launch App](https://ai-career-mentor-9xhvufgb2aksdg3jlknptm.streamlit.app/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; 💻 **GitHub:** [Repo](https://github.com/madasipallavi245-jpg/ai-career-mentor)
